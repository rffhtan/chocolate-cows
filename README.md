A more extended demo for my upcoming fuller game Chocolate Cows, which will be more of a tycoonish sandboxish game where you breed cows to create different drinks!

This establishes basic cow behavior (such as backflips and being led), cow genotypes, and the on click behaviors breeding, leading, and feeding.

Look forward to more features!!

# How to Play
PRESS SPACE TO SPAWN MORE COWS!!!

Click on cows to access their player-led behaviors, such as leading them, breeding and feeding them.

## leading
To lead them click them, click lead, then click somewhere else on the screen for them to go.

Trying to lead multiple cows at a time can lead to them moonwalking? not sure what that's about but it's an intentional feature...

## breeding
To breed them just click on one cow and then click on the other. 

Children will be the same species as one of the two parents they came from (TRUST ME. sometimes it feels like it's only giving one genotype but it's a 50/50)

Breeding is really simple, but more complex stuff like recessive traits or polygenic traits will ideally come in the full game.

## feeding 
To feed them, you just select which item from the three to feed them. 

This is the feature that will change the most obviously, in the future feeding cows will never change their species but it will influence their child's genotype! 

This iteration feature is more of a placeholder meant to showcase genotypes and different kinds of cows.
