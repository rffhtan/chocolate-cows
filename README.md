A demo of sorts for my upcoming fuller game Chocolate Cows, which will be more of a tycoonish sandboxish game where you breed cows to create different drinks! 

This establishes basic cow behavior (such as backflips and being led) and paves the way for other more central ones (breeding and being fed).

Look forward to more features!!

## How to Play

PRESS SPACE TO SPAWN MORE COWS!!!

click on cows to access their player-led behaviors, such as literally leading them (breeding and feeding behaviors not yet implemented).

to lead them, click them, click lead, then click somewhere else on the screen for them to go.
